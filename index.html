Short Prompt

I work in admin at an RTO. Write an email to a worried student explaining that their Statement of Attainment is delayed because of an issue with their USI. Tell them what they need to do next. Make it polite and clear.

Improved Prompt

I am a Student Administration Officer at a Registered Training Organisation (RTO). A worried student just visited the reception counter because they urgently need their Statement of Attainment for an employer, but we cannot issue it yet due to an unverified Unique Student Identifier (USI) name mismatch. Draft a supportive follow-up communication pack using the details below:

Instructions

  • Create two clearly labelled sections: 1. Student Follow-Up Email and 2. Internal SMS File Note.
  • In the email, acknowledge our conversation at reception, reassure the student, and explain in plain language that an RTO cannot legally issue a Statement of Attainment until their USI details match their enrolment record.
  • Provide clear, step-by-step instructions on how the student can check and update their personal details via the USI Student Portal, or confirm the exact ID document they used when creating their USI.
  • Clearly state what the student needs to reply with once updated, along with our expected turnaround time to issue their Statement of Attainment once verified.
  • In the internal Student Management System (SMS) file note, write a brief, factual summary of the counter enquiry, the advice provided, and a follow-up action item.

Behaviours

  • Maintain a warm, calm, empathetic, and reassuring tone in the student email to help ease their stress while keeping compliance requirements clear.
  • Avoid blaming the student or using heavy administrative jargon—keep explanations simple and action-oriented.
  • Keep the internal SMS file note objective, concise, and professional.

Other Considerations

  • Use [square brackets] as placeholders for all personal details (e.g., [Student First Name], [Unit/Course Title], [Admin Contact Number]) to protect student privacy when using AI.
  • Format the student action steps as a short numbered list so the email is easy to read on a mobile phone.
  • Include a brief checklist table at the end of the internal note showing what admin must verify before releasing the certificate.
  • Use Australian English (e.g., 'organisation', 'enrolment', 'apologise') and standard Australian VET terminology.
